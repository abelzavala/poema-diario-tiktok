## ✅ PROMPT PRINCIPAL — copia y pega esto

```
Cinematic vertical shot, pitch black background. An old thick leather-bound book rests
closed on a dark wooden table, lit only by a single warm beam of light falling from above
like light through a cathedral window, with dust motes drifting slowly through the beam.

The front cover opens AWAY from the camera and falls flat to the left side of the table.
The cover must never tilt up toward the lens and must never block the view of the book.
The pages then fan open gently by themselves, settling into a wide open spread.

The camera is locked off on a tripod and performs one single very slow continuous push-in
toward the open pages, until the aged parchment fills the entire frame at the end.

The pages are blank aged parchment, completely empty, no writing, no text, no letters,
no symbols, no illustrations.

ONE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE. No cuts, no jump cuts, no scene changes, no camera angle
changes. The same book, the same table, the same lighting from the first frame to the last.

Warm amber and cream tones against deep black. Volumetric god rays, shallow depth of field,
subtle 35mm film grain. No people, no hands, no camera shake.
Slow, reverent, meditative pace.
```

## 🔧 Si algo sale mal, agrega la línea correspondiente al final del prompt

**Si la portada sigue estorbando o se ve por atrás:**
```
The cover falls open flat and immediately out of frame to the left. Only the open pages remain visible.
```

**Si vuelve a haber un salto o cambia de toma:**
```
Single unbroken continuous shot, filmed in real time, no editing, no transitions, no cuts whatsoever.
```

**Si el libro se abre demasiado rápido:**
```
The entire opening motion takes the full duration of the clip, extremely slow and gradual, almost imperceptible frame to frame.
```

**Si la cámara se mueve o tiembla:**
```
Absolutely static tripod camera. The only movement is a slow smooth dolly push-in on a track.
```

**Si aparece texto en las páginas:**
```
The parchment pages are completely blank and empty, pure aged paper texture only.
```

**Si el final no llena bien la página:**
```
The final shot ends with the open right-hand page filling the entire vertical frame, brightly lit and centered.
```
